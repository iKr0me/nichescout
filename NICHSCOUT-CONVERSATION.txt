Product discovery is also the hardest to execute well and the most exposed legally. **Practical recommendation**

Start with product discovery as your core, layer in supplier scoring and margin analysis as the first two add‑ons, and treat the rest (ad optimization, pricing, cross‑channel sync) as later phases.
